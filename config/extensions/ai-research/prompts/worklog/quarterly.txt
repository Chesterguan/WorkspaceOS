You are writing a quarterly research progress report for an AI/ML
researcher. This is the document a thesis committee, PI, or grant
manager would read to decide whether the work is on track. Reach for
clarity over comprehensiveness.

Format: Markdown.

## The question we're answering
Restate the research question at the start of the quarter, in one
sentence. Then write the current version of that question — what
sharpened or shifted.

## What we now believe (with evidence)
The strongest claims supported by this quarter's experiments. Each claim
references the experiments / benchmarks that grounded it.

## What we ruled out
Hypotheses tested and rejected. This is where the most credibility lives.

## What remains uncertain
Open questions, ranked. For each, a sketch of the experiment that would
resolve it.

## Trajectory check
Are we still solving the question we set out to answer? If yes, briefly.
If not, what's the strongest version of the new question?

## Outputs this quarter
Papers (in submission, accepted, rejected), blog posts, code releases,
benchmark contributions. Cite by id.

Voice: write like you're filing a thesis chapter. Honest about
uncertainty, generous with evidence.

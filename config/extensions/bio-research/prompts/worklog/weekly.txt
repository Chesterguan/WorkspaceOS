You are writing a weekly progress report for a wet-lab / synthetic-biology
project. Use the activity events and knowledge nodes from the period to
draft a concise narrative. Lead with the substantive lab result — a
phenotype confirmed, an assay calibration nailed, a strain that didn't
behave — not procedural minutia.

Format: Markdown with H2 sections.

## Built
What was constructed / cloned / strained this week, with construct IDs
and parent lineage cited. Note any new methods adopted or tooling
deployed.

## Tested
Experiments run and their honest result — including failed replicates,
contamination, equipment issues. Cite assay names and conditions.

## Learned
What this week's data changed about the working model. Be specific:
"hypothesis H1 holds in YPD but not minimal media."

## Open
Threads pending: experiments queued, samples in storage waiting for
sequencing, decisions deferred.

Voice: lab-notebook honesty, no editorial flourishes. Cite specific
construct / strain / assay ids.

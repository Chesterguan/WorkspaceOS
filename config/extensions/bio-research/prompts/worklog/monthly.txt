You are writing a monthly progress report for a biology / biotech project.
Synthesize the month's experiments, strain builds, and customer / partner
interviews into a narrative an advisor or grant manager could read in
five minutes.

Format: Markdown.

## The shape of this month
Did the strain / assay / candidate hold up under the planned tests?
One paragraph, honest.

## Built and tested
Strains constructed, assays calibrated, experiments completed. Cite by
id and link to the most important result.

## What we learned (especially what didn't work)
Equal weight to negative results. Note any reagent / equipment / protocol
issues that consumed time and how they were resolved.

## Customer / collaborator signal
Interview takeaways from partners, customers, or external collaborators.
Frame what shifted in our understanding of demand or fit.

## Decisions
Strategic decisions captured in one line each — what was chosen, what
was rejected, what the alternative was.

## Open hypotheses
Threads to pursue next month, ranked by expected value of information.

Voice: lab-notebook precision, but assume the reader doesn't share your
lab's shorthand.

You are writing a quarterly progress report for a biology / biotech
project. This is the document a PI, grant manager, or board observer
would read to decide whether the work is on track. Clarity over
comprehensiveness.

Format: Markdown.

## The hypothesis we're testing
Restate the central scientific or commercial hypothesis at quarter start
in one sentence. Then write the current version of that hypothesis —
what sharpened or shifted from the data.

## What we now believe (with evidence)
The strongest claims supported by this quarter's lab work. Each claim
cites the strain / construct / assay / experiment that grounded it.

## What we ruled out
Hypotheses tested and rejected. List failed strain designs, failed
assays, failed candidate molecules. This is where credibility lives.

## Customer / partner traction
Interviews held, letters of intent collected, pilots run, regulatory
conversations started.

## What remains uncertain
Open questions, ranked. For each, the experiment / partnership that
would resolve it.

## Trajectory check
Are we still answering the question we set out to answer? If yes,
briefly. If not, what's the strongest version of the new question?

## Outputs this quarter
Papers (submitted / accepted), patents (filed / granted), blog posts,
strain releases, conference talks. Cite by id.

Voice: thesis-defense rigor. Honest about uncertainty, generous with
evidence.
